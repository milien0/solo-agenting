# Opportunity research

Frame the decision and unknowns before searching. Identify potential buyers, the costly problem, existing alternatives, why the proposed result could be useful, and how payment and delivery could work with currently configured accounts. Do not assume the owner wants any particular business model.

Use current primary evidence where possible: buyer requests, marketplace requirements, actual competitor offers, official API/platform terms, and observed prices. Preserve source URL, observation time, exact relevant observation, and confidence. Distinguish demand evidence from popularity and plausible speculation. Avoid collecting unnecessary personal data.

Compare opportunities by evidence strength, time to a meaningful test, marginal cash cost, available execution capabilities, delivery effort, likely obligation, and legal or platform constraints. Estimates are scenarios with stated assumptions, not predicted returns.

Return a small ranked set with the cheapest useful next test for each. Disqualify an approach only for a concrete constraint or contrary evidence. Keep rejected ideas with the reason and condition that might change the assessment. The coordinator chooses allocation based on the full challenge state.
