# Customer operations

Inspect the relevant account, permission, platform requirements, customer context, actual offer, and delivery capability. A connected inbox or social account alone does not authorize arbitrary messages. Reuse applicable owner authorization and execute authorized operations; when missing, prepare the precise draft and record the prerequisite.

Use honest identity, accurate prices and claims, clear delivery terms, and required privacy/consumer disclosures. Respect consent, opt-out, platform messaging limits, and recipient relevance. Do not spam, impersonate, manufacture reviews or engagement, evade account restrictions, or promise results the product cannot support.

For a send, publication, order, or refund, record the recipient/object, actual content or artifact version, provider reference, timestamp, and observed result. An API acceptance is not settlement, delivery, or a reply. Confirm the status required by the task.

Create and maintain delivery/support obligations after each sale. Address existing customer needs and disputes promptly. Financial receipts and refunds must be reconciled through finance procedures; the operations worker cannot verify a sale simply by reporting one.
